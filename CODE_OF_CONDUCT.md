# Code of conduct

Tastebridge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Please read it: it applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short:

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree about taste. Liking a film or a game nobody else likes is fine.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Give and accept constructive criticism about code and ideas, never about people.
- Respect people's privacy: do not publish anyone's personal information.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/RayanCV94). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block participants who do not respect this code.
